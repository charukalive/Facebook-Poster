📣 FB Poster

Write once → copy + open each group → paste & post

1. Paste / write your post in the editor and edit it.
2. Tap Copy + Open on a group — your text is copied and the group opens.
3. In Facebook: tap “Write something…”, long-press → Paste, add your image/video, Post. Come back and tap ✓.
Or: tick groups with the checkboxes and tap Copy selected group names + links — the old Copier list, with a live preview. The ↗ on a row just opens that group.
